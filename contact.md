## Frequently asked questions

### Which works are suitable for students?

Several works are suitable for beginners, students, and amateur performers. Bethan’s experience as a teacher informs her approach to writing for specific technical levels, and she is happy to discuss repertoire options or commissions tailored to particular educational contexts. Reasonable commission fees are available for student-level works — please get in touch for details.

### Can I reproduce scores or parts?

Scores and parts may not be copied or reproduced without written permission. Musical works are protected by copyright for the lifetime of the composer and for 70 years after their death. The Music Publishers Association issues a *Code of Fair Practice* covering copying and arranging of copyright-protected music. If you are unsure about permitted use, please contact Bethan and she will do her best to accommodate your needs.
